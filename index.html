Methodology
How DemandLens is built

Demand features

Lagged demand, rolling averages, rolling volatility, calendar effects and price signals are used to represent recent demand behavior.

Forecasting

A LightGBM regression model predicts next-period demand and is evaluated against a simple 7-day lag baseline.

Pricing analysis

Candidate prices are evaluated under multiple elasticity assumptions to compare expected demand and revenue.
